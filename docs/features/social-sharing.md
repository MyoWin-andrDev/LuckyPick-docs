#  Social Sharing & Viral Growth

Social sharing in LuckyPick is designed for organic viral growth while strictly maintaining user privacy.

---


---

##  Privacy Rules (Strict Requirement)

To protect user confidentiality:

*  **NEVER** expose real birth dates, family names, or pet names on generated share images.
*  **ONLY** display the final 5+2 generated numbers, the Lucky Mode badge (e.g. *"Birthday Mode"* or *"Zodiac: Leo"*), and the brand logo.
