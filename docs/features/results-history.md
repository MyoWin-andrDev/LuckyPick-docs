#  Results & Draw History

LuckyPick EuroMillions provides real-time access to EuroMillions draw results, breakdown of all 13 prize tiers, and a rolling 100-draw historical database.

---

##  Tuesday & Friday Draw Schedule

Official EuroMillions draws take place twice weekly in Paris at **20:45 CET** (Tuesdays and Fridays).

* Results auto-sync to the app within **15 minutes** of official publication.
* Displays main draw numbers, lucky stars, jackpot winner count, and rollover status.

##  Rolling 100-Draw Database

The app maintains a rolling history of the **last 100 EuroMillions draws**, allowing users to query past winning combinations, frequency distributions, and hot/cold number trends.
